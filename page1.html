What is the program and Movement about.

We help to build a prayer life for those who are not strong in Christ and are ready to give their all to God. This website is a medium that can help you get linked to mentors that would help you grow spiritually, through consistency in the place of prayer and also daily study of the word of God.

Also helps every teenager to build Godly friendships and relationships with their fellow teenagers that are rooted in Christ and can also help to buold themselves in the place of Prayer. And also help to grow and also know God more.

This movement would also help them discover their identities in Christ and know that God loves them no matter were they are coming from. And cause they are rooted in Christ and know their values in God, they don't need validity from social media or friends.

We also help to be focused on life and also to be guided by the word of God and not by the words of man and that they a great future ahead in Christ.
